# Robot dimensions: consult (2026-11-17)

You asked whether the Robot should own chords and keep an "alternate dimension" you can snap into; the show build freezes today and the Robot voice is unheard.

Problem it solves: a section change (who plays, which patterns, which chords) costs many keystrokes or a prepared state, and one player cannot do that in time.

Citations are `worktrees/v3-show-robot/` unless prefixed `v4:` (`worktrees/v4/`) or `root:`.

Two honest warnings:

- A "surprise me" key is a slot machine. The North Star asks for moves that cannot be played wrong, reward practice, and mean the same thing every time (`docs/NORTH_STAR.md:9-14`). Push the Harmony earns that through determinism. A generated world earns it only if the arrangement half is predictable.
- This is a third arranger beside states and auto-morph, and a second meaning of "Robot". Build it as the primitive the other two can sit on, or not at all.

## 2. The concept: Flip

"Jump" is taken by the Space leader (`runtime.rs:653`, `PERFORMANCE.md:269`). Call the move **Flip** and the candidate the **flip side**.

- **What it is:** a diff, not a state. A short typed list (layers in, layers out, patterns re-rolled, optional chord request) applied to whatever is live at the landing. A full snapshot (`session.rs:14-17`) would overwrite edits made while waiting and restart the clock.
- **Inputs:** layer audibility including physical holds, which the snapshot already carries (`session.rs:35-40`), levels, bars since the last flip, and the recently touched controls (`effect.rs:2507`) so it never moves what your hands are on.
- **Shape:** the arrangement half is a deterministic complement of what is sounding. Your example is exactly that: pads up and Kick+Bass out becomes Kick+Bass only. Dice apply only to layers that are entering, which are silent when rolled, so a roll cannot break a pattern mid-bar.
- **Keeps:** tempo, clock, the sounding chord, key, module chains, automation, gestures. Never silences everything.
- **Quantize:** next bar line for arrangement and patterns. Any harmony change follows the existing chord-window rules (`CONTEXT.md:89-94`), never the bar.
- **Holds win**, as on a state landing (`session.rs:324-327`). A Flip armed under a held `q` lands on the first bar after you let go: hold `q`, tap Flip, lift on the one.
- **Undo:** `/flip back` restores only the fields the flip touched, a few deep, live-only, like Push undo (`v4:docs/proposals/2026-11-11-v4-push-the-harmony.md:61,143`).
- **Preview:** one header line next to the state queue readout: `FLIP › Kick+Bass in · Pads out · Perc roll`, then `in 1 bar` once armed. Recompute when the snapshot generation changes or a phrase wraps, on the UI thread, not every beat. Pressing pins it: what you read is what lands.
- **Persistence:** the candidate is never saved. A landed flip is ordinary chosen state, so no new song record (Commandment 4).
- **Staying musical:** now, a small grammar of section moves (drop, strip, return) with invariants. Later, log every flip with "kept or reverted within 8 bars". That is a free label, and the journal route in the Robot proposal fits weights from it (`2026-11-17-robot.md:119-133`). The engine stays rule-based.

## 3. Robot-owned chords

Good when your hands are on rhythm and texture, and when eight chords have looped for ten minutes. Bad when you are playing Lead over them, mid-push, or relying on the authored key path between songs (`2026-11-02-show-readiness.md:194-205`). In ambient the progression is the song; re-rolling it is changing songs.

Control model: **you lead, the Robot answers requests**.

- `/chords new`, `/chords longer`, `/chords shorter`. Each lands at phrase end and is written as ordinary progression state.
- On v3 that means choosing among the 44 built-ins plus window and length, all existing controls (`voice/pad.rs:1005`, `registry.rs:1790-1844`); Master Key keeps any choice in key (`CONTEXT.md:76-79`).
- On v4 the Robot writes the ADR 0003 loop lane by chaining the same push moves you play. Your pushes and Vamp sit above it and always win; `G0` returns to the authored song (`v4:docs/adr/0003-chord-lane-and-clock.md:68-74,89`). "New total length" is the lane length.
- Any hand edit in a domain takes that domain back.

Do not hide a "re-roll the chords if they have played long enough" rule inside Flip. It makes one key mean two things.

## 5. Risks

- **Musical:** slot-machine feel; the complement becoming a gimmick; losing song identity; harmony cut mid-phrase.
- **Live:** preview changes between glance and press; hold interactions; accidental trigger in front of a room; terminals without key release.
- **Perf:** one large publish at a bar line inside a 256-frame, 5.3 ms callback (`root:...venue-rig-and-perf.md:120`). Gate on the callback overrun counter (9f3f0d9): zero new overruns.
- **Complexity:** a third arranger; chord generation on v3's eight slots is throwaway, since ADR 0003 step 6 breaks every code (`v4:...0003...:124`).

## Decisions only you can make

1. **Who leads.** Pull only (nothing lands without your key) / leases (hand a domain over, touch to take back) / Robot on a timer. Recommend pull only; leases at step 5; timer only inside auto.
2. **Does Flip touch chords.** Never / only when shown in the preview / hidden age rule. Recommend never: chords are their own move.
3. **Predictable or surprising.** Deterministic complement / seeded random world / both. Recommend deterministic arrangement, dice on entering layers only.
4. **Auto-morph's future.** Keep as is / rebuild on flips / delete. ADR 0003 already floats deleting it (`v4:...0003...:140-141`). Recommend freeze it now, rebuild at step 6.
