# Robot

Status: agreed direction from the owner (2026-11-17); v1 prototype on
`feat/robot`, not yet played or approved. Defaults below are proposed and
change only by playing. Off the show path: the 2026-11-22 set uses the pinned
v3 build.

## Intent

A Robot is a band member on its own layer and voice. It hears the chord that is
sounding now, improvises complementary lines that loop and develop, matches the
song's pace (slow, evolving song: long drawn-out notes), and never fights the
player's own knobs. Amount turns it on and up; Creativity and Boldness say how
it plays.

It is the rule-based first step of [AI Lead](2026-10-04-ai-lead.md). That
proposal's phrase planner (introduce, repeat, vary, answer, resolve), motif
record, and "nooise is the final musical safety layer" contract all hold. The
Robot ships the planner and safety layer now, with rules standing in for the
learned note generator.

## Prior art

| Work | What it does | Fit for v1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Melody RNN](https://github.com/magenta/magenta/blob/main/magenta/models/melody_rnn/README.md) | LSTM next-note melody model (basic, lookback, attention). | No. Needs a TensorFlow runtime and no harmony input. |
| [Improv RNN](https://github.com/magenta/magenta/blob/main/magenta/models/improv_rnn/README.md) | Melody RNN conditioned on the current chord (one-hot triad, or root + 12-bit pitch-class set). | Its chord encoding is the right input contract for a later model. The [Magenta repo](https://github.com/magenta/magenta) is archived and read-only. |
| [MusicVAE](https://magenta.withgoogle.com/music-vae) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05428)) | Hierarchical VAE over 2- and 16-bar loops; latent interpolation between phrases. | Best idea to borrow later: "Idea" as a point in a latent space, Creativity as distance from it. Offline today. |
| [Notochord](https://github.com/Intelligent-Instruments-Lab/notochord) | PyTorch real-time MIDI performance model. | Already proven over loopback in AI Lead. Python sidecar, not in-engine. |
| [MIDI-GPT](https://github.com/Metacreation-Lab/MIDI-GPT) | Controllable bar-infilling transformer. | Offline phrase proposals, not a per-note player. |
| [Impro-Visor](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~keller/jazz/improvisor/) ([source](https://github.com/Impro-Visor/Impro-Visor)) | Jazz solo generation from a user-editable grammar; grammars can be learned from a corpus of solos. | Closest analogue: rules now, rules learned from playing later. |
| [Markov chains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_chain#Music), [algorithmic composition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_composition) | Transition tables over pitch/rhythm states. | Cheap, but without phrase structure they wander. Useful later as a learned table inside the rules. |
| [Euclidean rhythms](https://cgm.cs.mcgill.ca/~godfried/publications/banff.pdf) | Even onset distributions (Toussaint). | Candidate rhythm generator for a later Boldness curve. |
| [Tidal](https://github.com/tidalcycles/Tidal) / [Strudel](https://github.com/tidalcycles/strudel) | Cyclic pattern transforms (rotate, degrade, palindrome). | The variation operators v1 uses (shift, thin, mirror) are the same idea. |

In-engine ML in Rust is possible later through [tract](https://github.com/sonos/tract)
(ONNX/NNEF), [ort](https://github.com/pykeio/ort) (ONNX Runtime) or
[candle](https://github.com/huggingface/candle). It stays out of v1: none is
needed for a rule player, and inference belongs off the audio thread one bar
ahead (AI Lead's runtime contract), which is scheduler work v1 does not need.

**Recommendation:** v1 is deterministic, rule-based and allocation-free on the
audio thread. Models come in through the corpus and planner below, not before.

## Musical rules (v1 as built)

- **Harmony:** reads `(progression, slot)` from its own `ProgressionFollower`
  and `pad_chord_tones`, the path Arp and Lead use, so it changes chord on the
  exact sample they do and follows Custom chords and Master Key.
- **Chord-tone targeting:** pitches index a ladder of the chord's pitch
  classes laid out upward from the robot's floor. Strong (even) slots sound
  chord tones. With Creativity, a weak slot can become the home-scale
  neighbour just above or below the next chord tone, so it resolves by step.
- **Phrase loops with variation:** an eight-slot phrase. Four phrases make a
  section: statement, restatement (more varied with Creativity), answer (the
  motif mirrored and displaced one or two slots; at low Boldness, half a
  phrase of space instead), and resolution onto the chord root. Low
  Creativity keeps one motif for four sections; high writes a new one each
  section. The contour steps toward a moving goal, so lines have shape.
- **Pace:** grid step = chord length / notes-per-chord (1 to 8, rising with
  Boldness), snapped to a power-of-two beat length of 0.25 to 8 beats and never
  under 0.15 s. Ties hold notes across slots; each note's envelope lasts its
  full tied length, so a 4-bar-chord ambient song gets notes lasting bars.
- **Register and range:** floor at the home tonic one octave up; Boldness widens
  the range from four to eight ladder rungs. Notes above MIDI 88 drop an
  octave.
- **Rests:** every phrase keeps at least one rest; density and sustain trade
  off with Boldness.
- **Determinism:** every note is a splitmix64 function of Idea, transport beat
  and controls. Renders repeat; a song code replays the same line whatever
  seed the session started with.

## Controls

| Row | Range, default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amount | 0-100%, 0 | Level and on/off. At 0 it does no grid work and allocates nothing. Also on the hub as the Robot row. |
| Creativity | 0-100%, 35% | Restatement variation, motif turnover, approach tones. |
| Boldness | 0-100%, 40% | Density, range, leaps, shorter notes, sharper attack. |
| Idea | 0-99, 0 | Seed for the motif family. Arrowing it is "another idea". |
| Type | Tonal synth types, Cloud Keys | Its own voice. |

Factory slot 1 is Room at 30%, so the Robot sits behind the player. Swing,
Drive, Delay and the rest come through `/` like on every layer.

**Persistence:** the codebase requires it. `song_ids_cover_every_registry_control`
forces every registry row into `SONG_ID_TABLE`, and the root rules say a
song save must restore chosen musical state. The Robot's ids are appended (no
reorder, no migration); a code without them loads with the Robot off. Because
notes are a pure function of controls and the beat, no runtime record is
needed.

## Not fighting the player

- **Own layer, own knobs:** it never writes another layer's controls.
- **Lead:** while the Lead lane is audible, the robot moves its floor out of
  the Lead's two-octave reach (Lead octave 0 puts the robot two octaves up)
  and keeps about 60% of its notes, dropping weak slots first.
- **Arp:** an audible Arp thins it to about 75%.
- **Human turn:** a played Lead key (`LeadPlayState` press) makes it stop
  starting notes until the end of the next phrase: call and response.
- **Mute** works like any layer (`m`, planned mute, Kick Only).

## Missing from v1 (next, in order)

1. **Listen and tune by playing.** Defaults (pace curve, densities, Room) are
   guesses until played over the built-in songs.
2. **Telemetry/OSC:** no `/nooise/voice/robot/note` events or visualizer
   ripple yet; level meter and layer state are wired.
3. **Melodic memory across sections:** v1 regenerates motifs from the seed; it
   does not recall an earlier section's motif later in the song.
4. **Listening to the Lead's notes,** not only its presence: answer the
   player's last phrase (transpose or invert it) instead of just yielding.
5. **Magnetization:** nudge a stagnant song toward a target state. This writes
   other layers' controls, so it collides with "never fights the player's
   knobs". It needs its own proposal: opt-in, through planned bar-boundary
   edits, cancelled by any human edit to the same control.

## Learning from the journals

The AI Lead recorder is the route: raw event log plus nooise harmonic context.
About 20 hours of journals is too little to train a note model from scratch,
but enough to fit the Robot's rule parameters:

1. Extract per-take statistics: notes per chord, chord-tone versus approach
   ratios per metric slot, interval histogram, rest lengths, register, repeat
   versus vary rates across phrases.
2. Fit them as a "style": the probabilities the rules use today, plus a
   small chord-relative Markov table for contour steps (Impro-Visor learns
   grammars from solos the same way).
3. A style is a data file chosen like a voice type; the engine stays
   rule-based and deterministic. A learned note model can replace the contour
   step later without changing the planner or the safety layer.

Annotation rules from AI Lead apply: human takes only, split by session, never
train on the Robot's own output.

## LFO / step-and-hold unification

The idea: an LFO `Steps` shape that holds pitch values gives one engine for
sequencers, LFOs and the Robot. Today the `Steps` staircase is a bipolar
modulation of a control in dial space (`automation/lfo.rs`). It is not
pitched, not chord-relative, and not a note source.

**Recommendation:** do not wait for it. The Robot needs pitches relative to the
sounding chord, ties, and phrase-level structure that a modulation lane does
not carry. If unification happens, the Robot's `RobotPhrase` (chord-relative
tone, tie, rest per slot) is a good candidate for the shared step type, and the
[melodic spike](2026-11-02-show-readiness.md#melodic-spike) (Melody 1/2/3, how
notes are produced separated from sound) is where that decision belongs. The
Robot becomes one more "how notes are produced" mode there.

## Acceptance

- Off by default: golden render and every built-in song unchanged
  (`golden_render_is_byte_identical_for_a_seed`).
- Amount 0: exact silence, no allocation
  (`amount_zero_is_silent_and_allocation_free`,
  `the_robot_plays_the_same_line_for_any_render_seed`).
- Same line for any render seed; a different Idea plays a different line.
- Strong-slot notes are chord tones; approach notes are scale tones within two
  semitones of the next chord tone; landings are the chord root
  (`notes_are_chord_tones_unless_they_approach_one_from_the_scale`).
- By ear: over a slow 4-bar-chord song at Boldness 40% it plays long notes
  that change with the chords; over a 124 BPM song at 80% it plays a
  recognisable two-bar idea that returns and resolves; with Lead playing it
  moves out of the way.
